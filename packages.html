Questions

How do I secure my date?

A signed agreement and a 30 percent retainer hold your date. The balance is due one month before the wedding.

Do you travel?

Yes, about half of my weddings are outside South Africa. I quote flights and accommodation at cost, with no extra travel fee for Europe or Africa.

Can we mix and match?

Of course. These collections are a starting point and I am happy to build something around your plans.

Will you send us every photo?

You get every photograph worth keeping, carefully edited. Blinks, duplicates and test shots are left out.
