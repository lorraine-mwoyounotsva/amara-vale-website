Galleries / Iglesia de San Pedro

Isabel & Marco

Candlelight, a gilded altar and a veil that ran the length of the aisle.

WhereIglesia de San Pedro, Cartagena, Colombia When22 March 2025 Guests210 SeasonDry season

The story

Isabel grew up a few streets from this church, and her grandmother was married at the same altar in 1961. The family asked for one thing: photographs that felt as warm as the room.

We worked almost entirely with the candles and the late light through the side windows, then slipped away for ten quiet minutes by a window before the reception.
